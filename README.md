# Healthcare Performance & Patient Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Healthcare Performance & Patient Analytics Dashboard is a Power BI project focused on analyzing the performance of a healthcare organization operating across different states in Nigeria.

The project was completed as part of my Data Analysis training journey, where I applied data cleaning, data modeling, DAX, data visualization, and business intelligence concepts to a real world style healthcare dataset.

The main objective was to answer:

**How is the healthcare organization performing, what are patients experiencing, and where are the major areas that require attention?**


##  Problem Statement

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of operational, financial, and patient-related data. Without proper analysis, it can be difficult for management to understand performance, identify problem areas, and make informed decisions.

This project solves that problem by transforming raw healthcare data into an interactive dashboard that provides a clear view of:

* Patient activity and demographics
* Hospital and departmental operations
* Revenue, costs, and profitability
* Waiting times
* Patient satisfaction
* Patient outcomes
* Performance across different states and departments

The dashboard allows users to interact with the data using filters and visualizations to quickly identify trends and areas that require attention.


##  Dataset

The dataset contains healthcare-related information covering patient visits and organizational performance.

Key data fields included:

* Patient information
* Age and gender
* Visit dates
* States and branches
* Departments
* Diagnoses
* Revenue
* Costs
* Waiting time
* Satisfaction scores
* Patient outcomes
* Revenue targets

The dataset covers healthcare activity during **2025**.

##  Analysis Performed

The project was structured around several key areas of healthcare performance.

### 1. Executive Performance

I created an executive overview to provide management with a high-level summary of the organization's performance.

Key metrics included:

* Total Patients
* Total Visits
* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Average Revenue per Patient
* Average Satisfaction Score

I also analyzed:

* Monthly patient visits
* Revenue by state
* Patients by department
* Revenue compared with target
* Patient outcome distribution


### 2. Patient Analytics

The patient analysis focused on understanding the characteristics and activity of patients.

I analyzed:

* Patient age
* Gender distribution
* Patient visits
* Patient distribution across departments
* Patient outcomes
* Average patient age
* Average daily visits
* Busiest departments
* Positive outcome rate

This helped provide a clearer picture of the organization's patient population and service demand.


### 3. Hospital Operations

The operations analysis examined how different departments and branches were performing.

I analyzed:

* Visits by month
* Visits by department
* Visits by branch
* Waiting time by department
* Average waiting time
* Department activity

This helped identify areas with higher patient traffic and potential operational pressure.


### 4. Financial Performance

The financial analysis focused on the organization's revenue, costs, profit, and target performance.

I analyzed:

* Total revenue
* Total cost
* Total profit
* Revenue by state
* Revenue performance against target
* Revenue distribution across different areas

This provided management with a clearer understanding of financial performance and where revenue was being generated.


### 5. Patient Satisfaction & Outcomes

I also analyzed the relationship between patient experience and healthcare outcomes.

Key areas included:

* Average satisfaction score
* Satisfaction by outcome
* Patient outcome distribution
* Positive outcome rate
* Satisfaction across departments

This helped highlight how patient experience varied across different outcomes and areas of the organization.

##  Key Insights

Some of the key findings from the analysis included:

* The organization generated approximately **₦44.45 million in total revenue**.
* The average patient satisfaction score was approximately **3.7/5**.
* The average waiting time was approximately **45.3 minutes**.
* The average number of visits per patient was approximately **2.43**.
* **Rivers** recorded approximately **₦12.73 million** in revenue, making it one of the strongest-performing states in the analysis.
* **Pediatrics** recorded **221 visits** in the analyzed data.
* Satisfaction scores varied across different patient outcomes, with scores generally falling within the **3.7 range**.
* The analysis of departmental activity helped identify the departments handling higher volumes of patient visits.

These insights demonstrate how healthcare data can be used to identify performance patterns, understand patient experiences, and highlight areas where management may need to focus.
